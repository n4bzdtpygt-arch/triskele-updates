Video effects, a new Program page, playbacks, and plot reading that checks itself

  • Video effects: video, picture, text and camera cues have an Effects tab: brightness, contrast, saturation (to black and white), hue, sepia, blur, vignette and a colour key for a green or blue screen, with ready-made looks. A fade cue's Effects toggle fades them (drift into black and white, blur out of focus), and fades a sound's built-in effects too (a reverb tail growing, a filter sweeping). The cue's own effects come back at its next GO.
  • Lighting, Program page: the keys are a full-height column on the right, the encoders run along the bottom, and the middle is four pools you choose from each pane's title tile (and how each looks: big or small tiles, a list, effects by type). Hide Keys (Tools menu, ⌃⌘K, the KEYS switch, or a MIDI pad) gives their space to more pools.
  • Playbacks view: ten faders to a page with GO, FLASH and REL, each holding a cue stack; every fader and button can be mapped to a MIDI controller and follows the page. TAP sets a tempo that effects can follow.
  • Effects: new Beam sweep (zoom, iris…) and Colour wave effects, a Fan knob that grows an effect along the lights, and Tempo to follow the tapped beat.
  • Light cues: right-click a cue in the cue sheet → Preview shows it on the plan and visualiser without touching the stage; Edit in Blind opens it in blind and Update goes into that cue.
  • Fixtures: Add Fixtures → New Type… makes a fixture type from its manual (starting from the one selected), saved on this Mac and exportable as GDTF; Request… asks for one or opens GDTF Share.
  • Import Rig: plots are now read from their own drawing lines, so each light is put on the pipe that actually runs through it (lights rated sure were right 9 times in 10 on nine venues' plots, up from 3 in 4). With the venue's paperwork, every light is checked against the plot: a strip says how many agree, disagree or are only on one side, and Review shows the differences side by side to choose from.
  • Exported plots now say Key to Fixtures.

A test show to try all of this (sound, video for a three-projector venue, and a lighting rig) comes as a separate folder.
