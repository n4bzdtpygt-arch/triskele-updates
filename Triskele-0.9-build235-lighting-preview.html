The iPad as a lighting wing, and touch screens

  • iPad lighting wing: turn on the phone remote (Setup → MIDI, OSC, Timecode & Phone Remote), open the /wing address it shows on the iPad and enter the PIN. Groups, palettes, keys and encoders by touch; everything happens on the Mac at once.
  • Touch mode for touch monitors (Lighting → Tools → Touch Screen): finger-sized keys, tiles and knobs, and ADD / REMOVE keys in place of ⇧ and ⌘.
  • Programming screen: smaller palette tiles, a roomier keypad, the selection tools above the groups, the encoders as devices along the bottom with a Programmer device (Fan, record mask, time, Blind).
