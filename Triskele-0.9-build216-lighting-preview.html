Sound editing, effects and the level line

  • Level line: in Trim & Loop, click Edit, drag across a stretch, then drag the line up or down.
  • Quick edits: select a stretch and right-click for Delete, Silence, Gain, Fades, Reverse, Copy/Paste, Split, Render to a New File.
  • Effects tab: EQ, filter, reverb, delay, compressor, limiter, pitch, distortion and Audio Unit plug-ins, with presets and Freeze.
  • Lighting: the Program view has a pan/tilt pad and an on-screen keypad; the visualiser has its own window.
