Recording Light cues by number

  • record cue 5 (or rec q 5) now puts cue 5 in the cue list in number order, between cue 4 and cue 6, whatever the standby is. Point cues too: record q 1.5 goes between 1 and 2. The command line says Recorded cue 5.
  • Number first, as on ChamSys: 5 rec or q 5 rec, then Return, records cue 5. On the keys: CUE 5 REC ENTER, or REC CUE 5 ENTER (pressing REC first now works).
  • A number a sound or video cue already has is refused, with a free number suggested; a Light cue with that number still asks merge, replace or remove.
  • With nothing set, record 5 makes an empty cue 5 to fill in later. q 5 on its own records nothing and says what does.
  • When the cue list can't take a recording (for example a locked show), the lighting window now says why.
