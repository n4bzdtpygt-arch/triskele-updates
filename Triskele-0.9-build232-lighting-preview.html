A new lighting programming screen, and editing on the waveform

  • Lighting, Program view: MagicQ's layout with grandMA3's look. Groups, colour, position, beam and FX palettes; MagicQ's keys on the right; the selection tools (Odd, Even, 1 in 3, Invert, Reverse, Rem Dim…) above the groups.
  • Encoders along the bottom, as devices (Intensity, Position, Colour, Beam) with a Programmer device for Fan, record mask, time and Blind.
  • Same keys everywhere: click uses a group or palette, ⇧ adds, ⌘ takes away, ⌥ stores. A line at the bottom says what anything under the mouse does.
  • Move in Black has a time per cue and finishes before the lights come on.
  • Sound: edit right on the waveform under a cue (no Edit mode): drag across to select, then Cut & Join, Silence or Gain; drag the white line to change the level. The waveform keeps its size.
  • Level line tools after Ableton: drag a segment, stretch and tilt handles, shapes, Simplify, Z/X zoom, R reverse, ⌘E split.
